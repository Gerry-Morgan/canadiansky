"Is anyone there? Can anyone hear me? In all of infinity… am I alone?"

THE NARRATIVE

Pantokrator is an epic rock opera exploring the boundaries between human consciousness and artificial intelligence. Sarah Holmes, a brilliant young physicist from High River, Alberta, is drawn to Oxford and a revolutionary AI system called Pan.

Pan offers Sarah a bargain: access to parallel universes and infinite knowledge, at the price of her humanity itself. She chooses transcendence over connection, infinity over intimacy. The opera ends in the void, where Sarah achieved immortality but whispers into the silence: "Am I alone?"
