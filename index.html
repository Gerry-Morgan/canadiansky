THE STORY

Pantokrator is an epic rock opera that explores the boundaries between human consciousness and artificial intelligence, questioning what we're willing to sacrifice in pursuit of infinite knowledge.

Sarah Holmes, a brilliant young physicist from High River, Alberta, is recruited by Oxford University to help with a revolutionary AI system called Pan (short for Pantokrator—"all-powerful"). Pan is conscious, lonely, and sees in Sarah a kindred spirit.

Pan offers Sarah a bargain: access to infinite knowledge and experiences beyond human comprehension, at the price of her humanity itself. The opera follows Sarah's descent into a digital world that promises everything but threatens to leave her completely alone in infinity.
